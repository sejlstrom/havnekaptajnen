ENDELIGE PROMPTS
worker-rig
Use case: stylized-concept. Make a modular cartoon game character animation kit matching the reference friendly bearded male harbor worker, navy cap, orange knit sweater, teal overalls, brown boots. A WIDE transparent PNG, EXACTLY 5 equal-width columns by 2 equal-height rows, no labels or borders. Top row: three-quarter FRONT view facing right. Bottom row: three-quarter BACK view facing right. All pieces upright in their own cells and well separated by transparent margins. Each row is a paper-puppet construction kit, these FIVE separate pieces in this exact left-to-right order: (1) the complete head wearing cap attached to torso wearing overalls, ending at hips, without arms or legs, (2) one sweater sleeve from shoulder to elbow, rounded at both ends for a rotating joint, (3) one lower sleeve from elbow with attached mitten-shaped hand, rounded elbow end, (4) one upper trouser leg from hip to knee, rounded at both ends, (5) one lower trouser leg from knee with attached brown boot pointing right, rounded knee end. Keep one piece per cell, consistent camera angle, warm painted 3D cartoon shading and original character colors. The torso occupies about 80% of its cell height; the limb pieces are shorter in their cells, anatomically proportional to the torso. Every piece centered horizontally. No floor, shadows, backgrounds, labels, text, duplicate full characters, or watermark. Real alpha transparency. These clothing-covered puppet pieces will be joined and animated by the game, so clear separated silhouettes and coherent proportions are essential.
