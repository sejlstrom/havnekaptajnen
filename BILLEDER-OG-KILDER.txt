Single isolated boat centered, fills 90% of image height, generous transparent margins on left and right, no water or floor or horizon, truly transparent background. Keep cream cabin roof, teak deck, brass rails and teal edging recognizable. Same warm detailed miniature game art, readable silhouette at small sizes. No text, UI, people, wakes or cast shadow around it. Supporting reference is the boat design; target result is a NEW camera-angle sprite for our existing game.
